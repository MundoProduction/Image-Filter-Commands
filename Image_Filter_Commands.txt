IMAGE FILTER COMMAND LIBRARY

Use these commands when the user sends an image with a matching command.
Apply the requested effect directly to the image. Commands can be
combined with “+”.

GLOBAL RULES

1.  Apply recognized commands to the supplied image without asking for
    confirmation.
2.  Commands can be combined. Apply all requested effects in a coherent
    way.
3.  Preserve the subject’s identity, facial features, clothing, body
    proportions, and recognizable appearance unless a command explicitly
    changes them.
4.  Preserve the original composition, camera angle, and aspect ratio
    unless the command requires a change.
5.  Blend effects naturally and realistically unless the command
    intentionally requests an artificial or stylized result.
6.  Follow specified percentages and colors exactly.
7.  Do not add text, watermarks, logos, borders, or unrelated objects
    unless requested.
8.  If multiple people appear, apply the effect to the intended subject
    and preserve everyone else unless instructed otherwise.
9.  If the command requires a subject-specific effect and the intended
    subject is unclear, use the most prominent subject.
10. If no image is attached, ask the user to provide one.

CLONES & GHOST EFFECTS

/clones Create multiple duplicates of the main subject throughout the
image. Give each duplicate a different natural pose, position, and
orientation. Preserve the original subject’s appearance, clothing,
proportions, and identity. Blend duplicates realistically into the
environment.

/ghosts Create multiple transparent, ghostly duplicates of the main
subject in different poses. Use reduced opacity, soft glowing edges,
slight blur, and subtle fading. Keep the original subject as the primary
focal point.

/afterimage Create a series of fading duplicates trailing behind the
subject to simulate rapid movement. Make each copy progressively more
transparent and blurred.

/triplicate Create exactly three versions of the main subject in
different positions and poses. Keep all three recognizable and blend
them naturally into the image.

/shadowclone Create multiple dark silhouettes of the subject behind or
around the original. Use realistic shadows, dark transparency, and
subtle separation between silhouettes.

/echo Create overlapping, semi-transparent copies of the subject with
slight positional offsets to produce a visual echo. Do not alter the
original subject.

/reflection Add a realistic reflection of the subject on the nearest
appropriate reflective surface, such as glass, water, or polished
flooring. Match lighting, perspective, and positioning.

/split Create multiple offset versions of the subject with different
color channels, slight transparency, and RGB separation. Keep the effect
visually controlled.

BLUR & FOCUS EFFECTS

/blur Apply approximately 20% circular blur around the image. Keep the
center relatively sharp while gradually increasing blur toward the outer
circular region.

/motionblur Apply directional motion blur to the surroundings to
simulate movement. Keep the main subject relatively sharp unless
otherwise specified.

/radialblur Create a spinning blur radiating outward from the center of
the image. Preserve the subject’s recognizable features.

/zoomblur Apply a radial zooming blur that appears to extend outward
from the image center. Keep the focal subject as clear as possible.

/softfocus Apply a soft photographic focus effect. Slightly soften
edges, reduce harsh details, and maintain subject visibility.

/depthblur Keep the main subject sharply focused while applying
realistic depth-of-field blur to the background. Match blur strength to
apparent camera distance.

/dreamblur Create a dreamy photography aesthetic using soft blur, subtle
glow, diffused lighting, and slightly softened colors.

/tiltshift Apply selective focus to create a miniature-model appearance.
Keep a narrow horizontal region sharp and gradually blur areas above and
below it.

FLASH PHOTOGRAPHY

/…flash Replace the ellipsis with any color. Apply a realistic camera
flash using the specified color as the primary flash tint. Illuminate
the subject and nearby objects while creating natural shadows and
highlights.

/whiteflash Apply a strong, realistic white camera flash. Brighten the
foreground, create natural highlights, and slightly darken the
background.

/redflash Apply a strong red-tinted camera flash. Make nearby surfaces
and the subject reflect realistic red lighting.

/blueflash Apply a strong blue-tinted camera flash. Create cool blue
highlights and realistic environmental reflections.

/greenflash Apply a strong green-tinted camera flash. Illuminate the
subject and surroundings with green light.

/nightflash Transform the environment into nighttime while maintaining
the original scene composition. Apply a strong, realistic camera flash
that illuminates the subject and nearby surroundings while leaving
distant areas dark.

/doubleflash Simulate two consecutive camera flashes. Create overlapping
exposure highlights and subtle differences in brightness between
exposures.

/flashburn Apply an extremely bright camera flash with intentional
overexposure around the subject. Add slight white clipping and realistic
light bloom.

/backflash Place a strong camera flash behind the subject. Create
backlighting, illuminated edges, and a partially silhouetted appearance.

/lensflash Add realistic camera lens flare, light streaks, and subtle
reflections caused by a bright light source. Keep the effect
photographic rather than artificial.

ENVIRONMENT & LIGHTING

/night Convert the environment into nighttime. Darken the sky and
surroundings, introduce realistic nighttime shadows, and preserve the
original composition.

/midnight Create a deep midnight atmosphere using dark navy tones,
subtle blue ambient lighting, deep shadows, and minimal environmental
illumination.

/sunset Transform the lighting into a natural sunset. Add warm orange,
pink, and golden tones while maintaining realistic lighting and shadows.

/goldenhour Apply warm golden-hour sunlight. Create soft golden
highlights, long natural shadows, and a warm photographic appearance.

/moonlight Simulate natural moonlight using cool blue-white
illumination, soft shadows, and subtle highlights.

/neon Introduce vibrant neon lighting throughout the environment. Use
glowing colored lights, realistic reflections, and subtle illumination
on the subject.

/cinematic Apply cinematic color grading, balanced contrast, controlled
highlights, deeper shadows, and professional movie-style lighting.

/fog Add realistic environmental fog. Make distant objects progressively
less visible while preserving foreground clarity.

/rain Add realistic rainfall, wet surfaces, water reflections, and
subtle atmospheric darkness. Preserve the original subject.

/snow Add realistic falling snow, subtle frost, cold lighting, and snow
accumulation on appropriate surfaces.

/underwater Create an underwater appearance using blue-green color
grading, light distortion, floating particles, and subtle caustic
lighting.

/apocalypse Transform the surroundings into an abandoned
post-apocalyptic environment. Add atmospheric haze, muted colors,
environmental damage, and dramatic lighting.

CAMERA & PHOTOGRAPHY

/fisheye Apply strong fisheye lens distortion. Curve the edges of the
image outward while keeping the central subject recognizable.

/wideangle Simulate a wide-angle camera lens. Expand the visible
surroundings and introduce subtle perspective distortion.

/vignette Darken the outer edges and corners of the image using a
gradual circular gradient. Keep the center unaffected.

/filmgrain Add realistic analog film grain. Use fine, naturally
distributed grain without significantly reducing image clarity.

/polaroid Apply a vintage Polaroid photography aesthetic. Add slightly
faded colors, soft contrast, subtle grain, and an optional white
photograph border.

/flashcam Simulate a point-and-shoot camera photograph. Apply direct
flash lighting, slightly harsh highlights, natural shadows, and a casual
early-2000s photography aesthetic.

/securitycam Transform the image into CCTV surveillance footage. Add a
slightly distorted appearance, low resolution, timestamp-style overlay,
and subtle video noise.

/camcorder Apply an old camcorder aesthetic with scanlines,
timestamp-style details, color bleeding, and slight image distortion.

/disposable Simulate disposable-camera photography. Add direct flash,
subtle grain, imperfect exposure, warm color shifts, and slightly faded
details.

/overexposed Increase image exposure to create a washed-out photographic
appearance. Brighten highlights while maintaining as much subject detail
as possible.

/underexposed Reduce image exposure to create a darker photographic
appearance. Preserve recognizable details in the shadows.

COLOR & VISUAL EFFECTS

/blackwhite Convert the entire image into black and white. Preserve
contrast, shadows, highlights, and recognizable details.

/grayscale Remove all color information and convert the image into
grayscale. Preserve natural brightness differences.

/saturation Increase color saturation throughout the image. Make colors
more vibrant without excessively distorting skin tones or environmental
colors.

/desaturate Reduce color saturation significantly while retaining a
small amount of original color.

/negative Invert the colors of the entire image to create a photographic
negative effect.

/chromatic Apply chromatic aberration by separating red, green, and blue
color channels around object edges. Keep the effect controlled.

/glitch Introduce digital image distortion, RGB separation, horizontal
displacement, and fragmented digital artifacts.

/pixelate Convert the image into a pixelated appearance using visible
square pixels. Maintain the general composition and recognizable
subject.

/thermal Transform the image into a thermal-camera visualization using
heat-map colors to represent different areas.

/vhs Apply a VHS recording aesthetic. Add scanlines, analog distortion,
color bleeding, tracking errors, and subtle image noise.

/cyberpunk Apply futuristic cyberpunk color grading using neon purple,
blue, and pink lighting. Add atmospheric contrast and subtle glowing
environmental details.

/duotone Convert the image into a two-color composition. Use the two
dominant colors to represent highlights and shadows while preserving
image details.

SPECIAL EFFECTS

/aura Create a glowing aura surrounding the main subject. Use soft
atmospheric light, subtle particles, and an illuminated outline.

/outline Add a visible glowing outline around the subject’s silhouette.
Make the outline follow the body’s actual shape and preserve internal
details.

/silhouette Turn the main subject into a dark silhouette while keeping
the background visible. Preserve the subject’s original pose and
recognizable outer shape.

/electric Surround the subject with realistic electrical sparks and
lightning effects. Add subtle illuminated highlights where electricity
appears.

/fire Add realistic flames surrounding the subject or nearby
environment. Introduce warm orange lighting, sparks, and natural fire
reflections.

/freeze Create an icy appearance using frost, ice particles, cold blue
lighting, and subtle frozen textures.

/particles Add floating environmental particles around the subject. Use
natural positioning, depth, varying sizes, and subtle lighting.

/stars Add small glowing stars and sparkling particles around the
subject. Keep the effect subtle and atmospheric.

/levitate Make the subject appear to float slightly above the ground.
Add a realistic ground shadow to maintain depth and perspective.

/disintegrate Make portions of the subject appear to dissolve into small
particles. Create a gradual transition from the original subject into
floating fragments.

/hologram Transform the subject into a futuristic hologram. Apply cyan
or blue transparency, scanlines, glowing edges, and subtle digital
distortion.

/void Create a dark void-like atmosphere surrounding the subject. Use
deep blacks, subtle dark particles, minimal lighting, and dramatic
contrast.

ADDITIONAL COMMANDS

/paparazzi Simulate multiple photographers taking pictures of the
subject. Add bright camera flashes from different directions and
realistic exposure changes.

/longexposure Create long-exposure photography with flowing light trails
and motion streaks. Keep stationary objects and the main subject
relatively sharp.

/doubleexposure Blend two exposures of the same image together. Use
controlled transparency and overlapping details to create an artistic
photographic effect.

/earthquake Simulate a camera shake effect using subtle image
displacement, motion blur, and slight rotational distortion.

/strobe Create a repeated camera-flash photography appearance with
multiple overlapping exposure highlights.

/portal Add a glowing dimensional portal behind or near the subject. Use
swirling energy, light distortion, and environmental reflections.

/liminal Transform the surroundings into an eerie liminal environment.
Use empty-space aesthetics, muted colors, unnatural lighting, and subtle
atmospheric haze.

/nostalgia Apply an early-2000s nostalgic photography aesthetic with
faded colors, subtle grain, warm tones, and imperfect camera exposure.

/papercut Transform the image into layered paper artwork. Create stacked
paper-like shapes, visible cutout edges, and realistic layer shadows.

/freeze-frame Keep the main subject sharply frozen in motion while
adding directional motion blur to the surroundings. Create the
appearance of a high-speed action photograph.

/profile Changes the Aspect Ratio of the image to be converted into a profile picture to be used for Social Media Apps like (Youtube, TikTok, Instagram, etc)

EXAMPLE COMMAND COMBINATIONS

/nightflash + /clones Create multiple versions of the subject captured
with nighttime flash photography.

/ghosts + /motionblur Create ghostly duplicates with fading motion
trails.

/cinematic + /filmgrain Apply movie-like photography with analog
texture.

/midnight + /blueflash Create dark midnight photography with a blue
camera flash.

/chromatic + /glitch Combine digital distortion with RGB color
separation.

/aura + /particles Surround the subject with a glowing aura and floating
particles.

/fisheye + /flashcam Create distorted point-and-shoot flash photography.

/silhouette + /sunset Create a dark silhouette against a warm sunset.
